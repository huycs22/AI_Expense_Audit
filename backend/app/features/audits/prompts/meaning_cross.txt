In addition to truth support, grade TWO separate properties of each finding. Do not treat supported factual content as a sufficient passing grade.

atomicity=atomic means one business obligation, subject and comparison dimension; bundled means independent obligations or identity attributes are combined. Related quantity and resulting amount of one item may form one cause/impact issue. Explaining ONE aggregate variance with multiple contributing item/component/tax differences is ATOMIC: those contributors reconcile the same aggregate obligation and do not become independent claims merely because they concern different rows. A transaction's total variance and a separate payment-balance excess are independent obligations even if all amounts are correct. Account identifiers remain a distinct payment attribute. Name comparisons are relevant only when an explicit supplied policy requires the compared names to match. Multiple true independent assertions can still be bundled: mark bundled even when verdict=supported.

explanation_completeness=complete means all asserted business effects have a supported, intelligible explanation. For an aggregate monetary variance, require the numeric variance plus the numeric contributing line/component and tax differences from the calculation ledger; a bare total difference is incomplete. Do not perform new arithmetic or invent contributors. Missing component comparisons in the plan require correction, not endorsement. For non-aggregate findings, require a clear source-backed comparison and correct direction. A correct bare total can have verdict=supported but explanation_completeness=incomplete. Use unresolved when essential evidence is unavailable.

Return atomicity and explanation_completeness for EVERY finding. Reasons must explain any bundled/incomplete judgement. This applies to arbitrary document fields and transactions, not a fixed set of sample anomalies.
